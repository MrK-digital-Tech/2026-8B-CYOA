Volcano Starts to Erupt

Patricia stood at the edge of the volcanic ridge, the warm ash-scented wind tugging at her dark hair as the sky glowed a deep, molten red behind her. Being Japanese, she’d grown up hearing stories about mountains with spirits and oceans with memories, and now she felt caught between both. To her left, the ocean shimmered like a sheet of silver calling her toward calm, open possibility. To her right, the rough, winding road carved through black rock and rising steam, promising challenge, change, and maybe danger. With the volcano rumbling beneath her feet, Patricia knew she had to choose follow the soothing pull of the sea or brave the uncertain path of the road each direction tugging at a different part of who she hoped to become.

Run to road Run to ocean
