Taxi to Cruise Ship

Patricia was jogging down the street, the cool morning air filling her lungs as she moved swiftly past familiar shops and houses. Suddenly, she spotted a yellow taxi waiting nearby and without hesitation, she waved it down and climbed inside. She told the driver to take her to the cruise terminal, her heart pounding with excitement and a hint of nervousness. Arriving at the bustling terminal, Patricia stood before two ships preparing to depart—one bound for the vibrant, colorful shores of Brazil, filled with lively festivals and lush rainforests; the other headed toward the ancient land of Egypt, with its mysterious pyramids and golden deserts stretching into the horizon.



Now, Patricia had to decide: which adventure would she choose to embark on? Would she lose herself in the rhythms of Brazil or uncover the secrets of Egypt? The choice was hers to make.
