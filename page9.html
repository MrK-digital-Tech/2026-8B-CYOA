Patricia in a cavern with phosporus walls

Patricia moved carefully through the cavern, her long black hair catching the soft glow radiating from the phosphorescent walls. The shifting green‑blue light reflected in her brown eyes as she walked deeper into the stillness, each step echoing faintly through the cave’s vast, breathing quiet. The glow around her pulsed gently, almost as if guiding her forward. Then, without warning, the light faded. Patricia slowed as she stepped into a pocket of darkness where the luminescence couldn’t reach. Before her rose a rough, shadowed rock face—an abrupt break in the smooth glowing surfaces she had followed. It looked ancient and untouched, a silent barrier holding secrets older than the cave itself, waiting for her to discover what lay beyond. Patricia lifted a hand toward the rough stone, her fingertips hovering just shy of its cold surface. The darkness pressed in thicker here, swallowing even the faint glow behind her, yet she felt something—an almost imperceptible warmth pulsing from within the rock, like a heartbeat muffled by centuries of silence. She leaned closer, steadying her breath as the air shifted around her, carrying a low hum that vibrated through her ribs. The cavern was no longer simply quiet; it was listening. And somewhere beyond that ancient barrier, something waited for her to take the next step.
