🪙Gold Is Too Heavy🪙

Patricia trudged across the pale stretch of sand, the gold she’d uncovered glinting fiercely in her hands. It felt unreal — heavy, warm, and full of promise. Every step forward made her imagine what this treasure could mean: security, a fresh start, maybe even a chance to fix the things her family had been struggling with for years. The wind pushed at her back as if urging her to keep going, to leave everything else behind and follow the path the gold had opened.

But then she stopped. Behind her, half-buried where she’d dropped it in her shock, lay her uncle’s notebook. Its worn leather cover was familiar, comforting, and full of the research and stories he’d spent a lifetime collecting. A page fluttered loose, skimming across the sand like it was calling her name. Patricia felt the pull of two futures — one glittering and uncertain, the other rooted in memory and meaning. The gold could change her life, but the notebook held the last pieces of someone she loved.
