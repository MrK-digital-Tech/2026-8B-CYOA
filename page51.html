Even though she was composed, it was obvious she was still just a teenager trying to do the right thing. The officers appreciated that she came forward, but they also noticed she was alone, with no parent or guardian in sight. Because of that, the situation shifted and the officers told her they couldn’t keep her there without an adult present.
