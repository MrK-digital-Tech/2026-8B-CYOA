Patricia on ride

Patricia stood at the entrance of her uncle’s favorite ride, feeling a mix of excitement and determination settle in her chest. She’d heard him talk about this ride for years, always with the same spark in his eyes, always insisting it was the best thrill in the entire park. Now it was finally her turn to experience it on her own, and the thought made her heart beat a little faster. As she stepped into the line, she imagined how proud her uncle would be when she told him she’d done it. The sounds of the park buzzed around her, but all she could focus on was the slow clim of the ride ahead and the adventure waiting at the top. When the safety bar clicked into place, Patricia took a steady breath. The moment the ride dropped, she burst into laughter—bright, fearless, and free—finally understanding exactly why her uncle loved it so much.
