See uncle walking into amphitheatre. Uncle gives speech about persistent nephews



Patricia paused at the top of the stone steps, the late afternoon sun spilling across the ancient amphitheatre. She hadn’t expected to see anyone she knew, let alone her uncle striding confidently across the stage as if he’d been waiting his whole life to stand there. For a moment she simply watched him — the sure way he moved, the easy command he had over the space, the familiar spark of mischief and purpose in his expression. Something about the scene made her chest tighten with a mix of pride and curiosity.

When he began to speak, his voice carried effortlessly through the open air. He launched into a story about the “persistent nephews” in his life — boys who refused to give up, who asked endless questions, who pushed boundaries until they carved out their own path. Patricia listened closely, knowing her uncle never told a story without a lesson tucked inside it. As the audience laughed and nodded along, she realised he wasn’t just talking about nephews at all. He was talking about anyone who keeps going, anyone who refuses to let the world quiet their determination. And in that moment, she felt as though the message was meant for her too.
