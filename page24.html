Arrested and Deported

Patricia noticed a suspicious man following her uncle. She headed straight to the local police station to report what she had observed. At the police station, Patricia calmly explained everything she had seen. Her clear testimony, the police quickly reviewed, helped them identify and track down the man. Then officers arrested the follower near her uncle’s workplace. It turned out he was an undocumented individual with a history of suspicious activity. After a swift investigation, the authorities deported him from Japan.
