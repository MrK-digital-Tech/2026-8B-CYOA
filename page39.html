Seeing A Man With Uncle's Hat

Feeling uneasy after spotting the man wearing her uncle’s hat, Patricia quickly stepped away from the crowd and called the police. Her hands trembled slightly as she explained where she was and described the sketchy man she had seen near the markets in New Delhi. She tried to stay calm while giving details about his appearance and the direction he had walked in, hoping the officers would take the situation seriously. As she waited for help to arrive, Patricia kept watching the busy streets carefully, worried that the man might disappear before anyone could question him.

Patricia kept several metres behind the mysterious man as he moved through the crowded streets of Las Vegas. Trying not to draw attention to herself, she blended into the sea of tourists and bright neon lights while carefully watching his every move. Her heart pounded each time he glanced over his shoulder, and she quickly pretended to look at the shops around her. Although fear crept into her mind, Patricia stayed determined, convinced that following the man wearing her uncle’s hat could finally lead her to the truth about his disappearance.
