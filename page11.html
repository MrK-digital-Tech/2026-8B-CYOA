Clue Leads To Pyramid

Patricia stood in the Louvre’s vast gallery, her long black hair falling over her shoulders and her warm brown eyes fixed on the worn book in her hands. Her red long sleeved shirt stood out against the cool marble surroundings, and her beige cargo pants brushed softly as she shifted her stance. The white Nike shoes on her feet made faint squeaks on the polished floor, and her black backpack rested snugly against her back. Inside the old book, a circled line of text read: “The path begins at the pyramid.” She lifted her gaze toward the famous glass Louvre Pyramid just outside, feeling the weight of the clue settle over her.

But the message wasn’t clear, and that uncertainty tugged at her. Did the clue mean the modern pyramid only steps away, or was it pointing her toward the ancient pyramids of Egypt, rising from the desert under a blazing sun? Patricia imagined both scenes vividly—the shimmering glass structure in Paris and the monumental stone giants of Giza. Each possibility felt like the start of a different adventure. Standing in the quiet hum of the museum, she felt suspended between two worlds, unsure which path the clue truly wanted her to follow.
