Run As Fast As You Can

Security cornered Patritca before she even had a chance to catch her breath. Two guards stepped out from behind the metal detectors, their expressions sharp and suspicious, blocking both exits as the alarms blared overhead. They demanded to know what she was carrying, why she was running, and what exactly she thought she was doing in a restricted area. Patritca’s mind raced—she didn’t have the package anymore, and the truth sounded unbelievable even to her. She needed something, anything, to prove she wasn’t the threat they thought she was.

Her only hope was her uncle’s old book—the one filled with strange notes, codes, and explanations that might finally make sense of everything that had happened. If she could convince them the book mattered, that it explained why she was being chased, maybe they’d listen long enough for her to escape. But time was slipping away, and the guards were growing impatient. She had to decide her next move immediately: whether to fly to Canada or fly to Egypt.
