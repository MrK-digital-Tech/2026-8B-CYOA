Patricia watching an all Black games

Patricia found herself pressed up against the railing of the stadium, eyes wide as she watched the all‑black‑uniformed rugby team thunder across the field. The energy was different from anything she’d felt before—raw, fast, and almost overwhelming. Every time the players collided, the sound echoed through the stands like a drumbeat, and she felt it in her chest. She didn’t know all the rules, but she didn’t need to; the intensity of the match pulled her in on its own. The crowd roared with every sprint, every tackle, every near‑miss, and Patricia couldn’t help but get swept up in it. As the game went on, she started to notice the rhythm behind the chaos—the way the team moved almost like a single organism, each player anticipating the next step before it even happened. Their coordination fascinated her. She leaned forward, completely absorbed, cheering when the team broke through the defensive line and surged toward the try zone. For a moment, she forgot everything else—the noise, the crowd, even the world outside the stadium. All that existed was the game, the players in black, and the electric thrill of watching something powerful unfold right in front of her.
