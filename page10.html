Mountie has seen Patricia's uncle

Patricia grew nervous after hearing that the Mounties, the Royal Canadian Mounted Police, had seen her uncle earlier that week. Nobody knew where he was headed, and no one had heard from him since. Patricia was told that she could either stay at a hotel in Quebec City and wait for more information or travel to Banff in hopes of finding him there. She sat quietly for a long time, unsure which decision to make.
