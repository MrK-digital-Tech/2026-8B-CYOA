Back in Australia and Uncle is looking for bunyips

Patricia barely had time to drop her suitcase in the back of uncle's ute before he was already pulling out of the airport car park, grinning like a kid who’d just been handed a treasure map.



“Straight to the wetlands,” he said. “Perfect day for bunyip hunting.” The city slipped away behind them, replaced by open fields and thick bushland. Patricia pressed her face to the window, excitement buzzing in her chest. She’d heard her uncle 's stories since she was little—strange splashes at dusk, deep tracks in the mud, eerie calls echoing across billabongs. Whether bunyips were real or not, she was finally here, chasing the mystery with him. They parked at the edge of a quiet wetland. The air was warm, still, and humming with insects. Her uncle handed her a flashlight. “Ready, Patty? Let’s see what’s out there.” And just like that, they stepped into the reeds together, hunting bunyips side by side.
