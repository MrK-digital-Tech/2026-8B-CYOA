Patricia at the Airport

Patricia stands at the edge of the bustling airport terminal, her long black hair falling like a curtain she keeps tucking behind her ear whenever her nerves spike. Her brown eyes track every movement around her—families reuniting, business travelers striding with purpose, the distant rumble of a plane taking off. Dressed in a red long‑sleeved shirt, beige cargo pants, and crisp white Nike shoes, she looks like any other teenager heading off on an adventure, but her fingers twist anxiously around the strap of her backpack. Flying has always felt like stepping into the unknown, and today that unknown feels especially large.

As Patricia leaned closer, she noticed a faint symbol hidden within the dark background of the painting. Her heart raced as she carefully copied the strange markings into her notebook. After comparing the symbols with an old guidebook she carried in her backpack, she realized they formed part of a hidden message left centuries ago. The discovery filled her with excitement and nervous curiosity. Standing beneath the soft museum lights, Patricia knew she had uncovered a mystery connected to the Leonardo da Vinci himself, and it was only the beginning of an unforgettable adventure.
