Overhear men talking about Uncle's location

Patricia was heading home from school when she slowed near the corner store, noticing two men standing beside a parked car. Their voices were low, but one name cut through the air like a spark: her uncle’s. She froze, hidden behind a street sign, as the men mentioned a location she’d never heard before—somewhere “off the main road” and “far enough that no one would stumble onto it.” They spoke like they weren’t supposed to be overheard, glancing around as if the whole street might be listening. Patricia felt a chill crawl up her spine. Why would strangers know where her uncle was going, and why did they sound so sure he wouldn’t be followed?

She backed away slowly, her heartbeat loud in her ears. The men kept talking, tossing around details that made her stomach twist—timing, directions, and a warning that “he better show up alone.” Patricia didn’t understand what any of it meant, but she knew one thing: her uncle hadn’t told anyone in the family he was going anywhere. The secret she’d stumbled into felt heavy, like a door she wasn’t meant to open. As she hurried down the street, the name of that strange location echoed in her mind, leaving her torn between telling someone and trying to uncover the truth herself.
