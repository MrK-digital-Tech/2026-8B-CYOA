Kidnappers Caught with Uncle in Hideout

Patricia stood in the dimly lit hideout, her long black hair hanging in loose strands around her face as she tried to stay calm beside her uncle. The red sleeves of her shirt were dusty from being dragged inside, and her beige cargo pants were creased from hours of tension. Her brown eyes—usually bright with teenage energy—were sharp and alert now, scanning every shadow for danger. Even her white Nike shoes, once spotless, were scuffed from the struggle. Despite the fear pressing in from the cold concrete walls, she stayed close to her uncle, drawing strength from his steady presence as they waited for a chance to escape.
