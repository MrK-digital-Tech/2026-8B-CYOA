Floor opens up and you slide out into desert

The dim chamber inside the pyramid was thick with dust and silence. As she crept forward, torchlight flickering across the walls, the stone floor suddenly cracked open with a deep grind. She screamed, arms flailing, but the ground gave way beneath her. Her fingers scraped desperately at the crumbling edge for a split second before she plummeted into the dark, bottomless pit below.

She plummeted into the dark pit, tumbling through cold emptiness, until the chute violently spat her out into blinding sunlight. She crashed hard onto the warm desert sand right outside the pyramid.
Dazed, she pushed herself up and looked around. Behind her loomed the pyramid’s dark entrance once more. Ahead, across the dunes, the massive Sphinx waited with its mysterious opening at the base. Heart still racing, she had to choose:

slip back into the pyramid or head toward the Sphinx.
