As you drag along corridor floor turns to slide

I dragged the large wooden chest slowly along the corridor floor, the metal corners scraping loudly against the tiles with every step. My arms ached from the weight of it, but I kept pulling, my white sneakers squeaking slightly as I moved. The hallway stretched on forever beneath the dim golden lights above, and I glanced around at the old paintings and doors lining the walls, wondering what could possibly be hidden inside the chest. My long black hair kept falling into my face, and I pushed it back with an exhausted sigh before tugging the heavy thing forward again.

Then, without warning, the smooth floor ahead of me suddenly bent downward and transformed into a giant slide. The tiles curved sharply into the ground as if the corridor itself had melted into some strange amusement ride. I froze instantly, my hands still gripping the chest handle as my eyes widened in disbelief. My heart hammered against my chest while I stared at the impossible sight in front of me, completely shocked. The chest rested beside me in silence, and for a few seconds I could do nothing except stand there, confused and speechless, wondering if I was imagining the whole thing.
