Other Arc de Triomphe in New Delhi

Patricia, a Japanese teenage girl with long black hair and brown eyes, wandered through the crowded streets near the Other Arc de Triomphe in New Delhi, searching for her missing uncle. Dressed in a red long-sleeved shirt, beige cargo pants, and white Nike shoes, she pushed through the busy crowds with determination despite feeling anxious and tired. The noise of traffic and the unfamiliar city around her made the search overwhelming at times, but she stayed focused, carefully looking at every street and every passerby, hoping for any sign that could lead her to him.

As she wandered through the bustling streets of New Delhi, her eyes scanning every face in the crowd as she searched for her missing uncle. The city pulsed around her—vendors calling out over the honking traffic, the scent of spices drifting through the warm air, and bright colours flashing from every shopfront. Though the noise and movement felt overwhelming, she kept moving with quiet determination, clutching a small photograph of him in her hand. Each turn down a narrow lane, each question she asked strangers, carried a mix of hope and worry, but she refused to give up. In a city as alive and unpredictable as Delhi, she believed that somewhere in the maze of people and stories, she would find him.
