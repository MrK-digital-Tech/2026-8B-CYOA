Escaping the Gas


Patricia didn’t hesitate as the gas began to pour into the chamber, rushing toward the left door and forcing it open. For a split second, she expected another passage—but instead, there was nothing but empty space. She stumbled forward and fell, tumbling out of the pyramid entrance and crashing hard onto the stone and sand below. A sharp pain shot through her leg, and she cried out, unable to stand as the reality of the injury set in.
An ambulance arrived soon after, its siren cutting through the heat and noise as she was rushed to the nearest hospital. Doctors confirmed her leg was broken and set it in a full plaster cast. Exhausted and disappointed, Patricia was later taken straight to the airport, her adventure cut short. As she boarded the flight back to Australia, she stared out the window, frustrated by how quickly everything had gone wrong—but knowing the mystery wasn’t over yet.
