Suddenly, a man dressed entirely in black rushed past from behind, his hand swiftly snatching the notebook from her grasp. She gasped in shock, her brown eyes widening as she spun around and reached out desperately, but he was already weaving fast through the pedestrians, disappearing into the bustling crowd with her notebook clutched under his arm.
