At the Hotel


Patricia stood at the front desk, brushing a few snowflakes from her sleeve as she spoke to the receptionist. The warm glow of the Banff hotel lobby felt a world away from the cold outside, but her thoughts were fixed on one thing—her uncle. She explained that Uncle George had stayed there recently, and after a quick check on the computer, the receptionist confirmed it, even giving her the room number. “He didn’t stay long,” she added, lowering her voice slightly, “but he did speak with our concierge before he left.” Patricia’s pulse quickened. She was so close now—closer than she had been since finding the notebook
She glanced toward the lift, imagining what might still be waiting in that room—notes, clues, or something he had left behind in a hurry. But then she looked across the lobby at the concierge desk. If Uncle George had spoken to someone before leaving, there might be information that wasn’t written anywhere. Patricia hesitated, caught between acting quickly and gathering more details. Booking the room could give her immediate access to physical clues, but talking to the concierge might reveal something far more important. She took a steady breath, knowing this decision could shape what happened next.
