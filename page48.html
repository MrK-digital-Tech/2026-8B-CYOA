Security catches Patricia

Patricia didn't get far before security caught her, stopping her right where she stood. One of the guards stepped in front of her, his expression unreadable as he asked why she was out on her own. Her breath hitched, and she froze, feeling the weight of his attention settle on her like a heavy coat she couldn't shrug off. He didn’t raise his voice, but the firmness in it made her stomach twist. She tried to explain herself, but the words tangled on her tongue, coming out thin and shaky. The guard's stance didn't soften, and Patricia felt her pulse thudding in her ears as he told her she needed to stay put until someone could confirm who she was. Standing there, caught and unable to move, she felt the fear creep up her spine, making her wish she had never tried to slip away in the first place.
