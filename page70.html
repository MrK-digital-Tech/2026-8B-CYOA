Waving Down A Passing Police Man

A girl hurried along a busy Las Vegas sidewalk, lifting her hand to wave down a police officer as the neon glow washed over the street. She wasn’t trying to get in trouble she just needed help. Her uncle had vanished somewhere in the endless maze of casinos, hotels, and crowds, and she’d been searching for what felt like hours. The officer’s attention finally shifted toward her, and she tried to steady her voice, hoping he’d understand the urgency behind her small, nervous gesture. In a city built on noise and spectacle, her quiet determination stood out, driven by the hope that someone in uniform might help her find the one person she was desperate to bring back.

The officer eventually decided the safest thing was to send her home, but “home” wasn’t just a short drive away. As she stood beside the patrol car, he explained gently that arrangements had been made for her to fly back—someone would meet her at the airport, and she’d be taken straight onto a plane. The news hit her like a cold wave. She wasn’t ready to leave Las Vegas, not while her uncle was still missing somewhere in its bright, chaotic streets. But with the city lights flickering behind her and the officer insisting it was for her own safety, she found herself walking toward the terminal. The hum of the airport felt strange and distant, and as she clutched her boarding pass, she stared out at the runway, wishing she could turn around and keep searching. Even as the plane prepared for takeoff, her thoughts stayed anchored to the one person she still hadn’t found..
