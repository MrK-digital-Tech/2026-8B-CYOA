Note for Statue Cleaner

Patricia stood beneath the towering Christ the Redeemer statue, clutching a small folded note meant for the statue’s cleaner. She knew there was only one maintenance door she could realistically reach—the one leading into the statue’s left hand. It wasn’t a tourist attraction, and she wasn’t supposed to enter it at all, but the thought of slipping inside and leaving the note where the cleaner would find it tugged at her curiosity. The left hand overlooked the endless stretch of ocean, and Patricia felt the weight of the forbidden choice settle over her as she stared at the narrow, hidden doorway.

But another option tugged at her mind: she could simply turn around and head back to her hotel. The path down the mountain was familiar and safe, lit by soft lanterns that would guide her all the way to her room. A warm shower, a quiet bed, and the comfort of routine waited for her there. Part of her wondered if she was overthinking everything—if delivering the note could wait, or if it even mattered at all. As the breeze brushed past her, Patricia found herself caught between curiosity and comfort, unsure whether to chase the unknown or retreat to the peaceful certainty of her hotel room.
