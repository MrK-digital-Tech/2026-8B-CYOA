Chase Through Hawaii

Patricia sprinted down the sun soaked boardwalk, her sneakers slapping against the wood as the Hawaiian breeze whipped past her. She wasn’t usually the type to chase anyone but today was different. The stranger weaving through the crowd had something she couldn’t afford to lose—a small, leather notebook packed with maps,cryptic clues, and grainy photos she’d collected over months. Tourists stepped aside as she pushed forward, her heart pounding harder than the surf crashing against the shore.



The chase carried her off the boardwalk and onto a narrow path lined with hibiscus and palm trees. Patricia could see the thief glancing back, clearly surprised she was still on his heels. Determination fuelled her; this wasn’t just about a notebook, it was about protecting the trail of discoveries she’d been following. As they neared a quiet stretch of beach, she felt the gap closing. The sun glinted off the ocean, and for a moment she wondered how her peaceful vacation had turned into a full blown pursuit— but she wasn’t stopping until she got that notebook back.
