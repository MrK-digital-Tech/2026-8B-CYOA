Gold cavern

Patricia stepped into the gold cavern as though crossing the threshold of a dream. Light rippled across the walls in molten waves, each surface catching her reflection and stretching it into something tall and ancient. She brushed her fingers along a pillar of raw gold, surprised at how warm it felt—almost like a heartbeat pulsed beneath the metal. The air shimmered with drifting flecks of gold dust, swirling around her like tiny stars that had lost their sky.

Deeper inside, the cavern opened into a vast chamber where mountains of coins and relics lay half-buried in glittering sand. But Patricia’s attention was drawn to a soft glow at the center, a gentle pulse that seemed to call her by name. She stepped toward it, feeling the ground hum beneath her feet. Whatever waited in that light wasn’t just treasure—it felt like a message, a secret meant only for her, something that might change the path of her life forever.
