AVALANCHE!!!

Patricia pushed through the thick snow with determination burning in her brown eyes. Her long black hair whipped across her face as icy wind howled through the mountain pass. Dressed in a red long-sleeved shirt, beige cargo pants, and white Nike shoes already soaked from the freezing snow, the Japanese teenager followed the trail of two sketchy men she had spotted near the abandoned ski cabins earlier that evening. They kept disappearing into the blizzard ahead of her, their dark figures barely visible between the falling snow and jagged rocks. Patricia’s breathing grew heavier as the mountain became steeper, but she refused to stop chasing them.

Without warning, a deep crack echoed across the slope. Patricia froze as the snow beneath her trembled violently. A massive avalanche broke loose high above the mountain, sending a roaring wall of snow crashing toward her at terrifying speed. She tried to run, but the force of the avalanche swept her off her feet instantly, tossing her through freezing snow and shattered ice. The cold swallowed her whole as the deafening roar surrounded her.
