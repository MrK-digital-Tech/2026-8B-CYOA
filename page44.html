Patrica walked along the beach with the steady rhythm of the waves beside her, her long black hair brushing against the red sleeves of her shirt. The cavern entrance she had discovered earlier sat just a few steps back from the shoreline, hidden by shadows and the curve of the rock. Inside it, she had found the massive chunk of gold—so bright it looked unreal, so heavy it nearly pulled her white Nike shoes straight into the sand when she tried to lift it. Even with her beige cargo pants braced and all her strength behind it, the gold refused to move without dragging her down.

Patrica stood where the waves lapped at the edges of her white Nikes, the sand shifting softly under her soles as she stared back at the spot she’d uncovered. Her mind churned with possibilities she wasn’t ready for. Should she tell someone and risk the whole thing being taken out of her hands, or bury it again and hope the ocean or time would swallow the secret for her? Neither choice felt safe. She crossed her arms tightly, the wind tugging at her hair, wishing the answer would rise out of the tide instead of sitting heavy in her chest.
