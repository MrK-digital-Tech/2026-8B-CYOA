Message in Notebook but is it North or South?

Patricia flipped open her notebook, the pages fluttering as though they were breathing. The message was still there—urgent, slanted handwriting she knew wasn’t hers: “Go now—before the path closes. But is it north or south?” Tucked beside it were two travel pamphlets she had no memory of collecting. One pointed her toward New England, America, a northern land of storm‑lashed coastlines, old secrets, and towns that seemed to watch newcomers with quiet curiosity. The other urged her toward New England, Australia, far to the south, where wide plains met ghost‑gum forests and stories lingered in the dust like footprints that refused to fade.

The air around her felt charged, as if the choice itself carried consequences she couldn’t yet see. The notebook seemed to wait, almost alive, ready to shift her fate the moment she chose a direction.
