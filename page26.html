Back home paying off tickets

Patricia, a spirited Japanese teenage girl with long, silky black hair and warm brown eyes , sat at the kitchen table in her family’s Tokyo apartment, her fingers nervously tapping the screen of her phone. She wore her favorite red long-sleeve shirt, the one that always made her feel bold, paired with her trusty beige cargo pants and white Nike sneakers—still scuffed from the cobblestone streets of Paris.
Her heart pounded as she scrolled through her bank account, calculating how much she could send to her uncle. She wasn’t supposed to be in France last month, but when she heard about his sudden trouble, she couldn’t just stand by. Now, back home, she was determined to pay off the tickets and fines, even if it meant skipping her favorite ramen shop for weeks. With a deep breath, she clicked “transfer,” hoping her uncle would never find out just how much she had sacrificed for him.
