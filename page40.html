Las Vegas

Patricia stepped out into the bright, buzzing energy of Las Vegas, her suitcase still in hand and her mind already racing with possibilities. Neon lights flashed even in the daytime, and everything felt larger than life — but her thoughts were somewhere else entirely. She had a choice to make, and it felt like a big one. Should she head to Disneyland in California, the original park she’d seen in so many old photos and videos, or fly across the country to Walt Disney World in Florida, the massive, magical world that seemed to go on forever?

She leaned against her suitcase, imagining both options. California felt classic and cozy, like stepping into a piece of history where everything began. Florida, on the other hand, sounded like a full-on adventure—multiple parks, bigger rides, and days of exploring without seeing it all. Patricia bit her lip, smiling to herself as she pictured castles, fireworks, and meeting her favourite characters in either place. It wasn’t really about which one was better—it was about what kind of experience she wanted right now. With a deep breath, she grabbed her phone, ready to make a decision that would turn this trip into something unforgettable.
