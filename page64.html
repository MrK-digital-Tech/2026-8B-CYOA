Going to the border to meet Hill Tribe

Patricia had always heard stories about the Hill Tribe—whispers of vibrant markets, mist‑covered mountains, and people who carried centuries of tradition in their voices. But she never imagined she’d be standing at the edge of the border, dust swirling around her shoes, with her Uncle Rami smiling like he’d been waiting for this moment her whole life. He greeted her with a warm, grounding hug, the kind that made the long journey feel suddenly worth it. “Ready?” he asked, not as a question but as a promise. And with a nod, Patricia followed him toward the narrow path that led deeper into the highlands.

As they approached the border crossing, Patricia felt the world shift. The air grew cooler, carrying the scent of woodsmoke and wild herbs. A small group from the Hill Tribe waited on the other side—men and women dressed in hand‑woven fabrics, colors bright enough to rival sunrise. One elder stepped forward, eyes kind and curious, and Patricia felt something inside her settle. This wasn’t just a meeting; it was an invitation. Her uncle placed a gentle hand on her shoulder, guiding her forward. In that moment, Patricia realized she wasn’t just visiting a new place—she was stepping into a story that had been waiting for her all along.
