Overcome by smoke. Found and sent to hospital

Patricia stood near the smoking hillside, her long black hair sticking to her face as the air grew thick with ash. Her brown eyes watered from the sharp sting of the smoke, and she tried to shield her mouth with the sleeve of her red long sleeved shirt. Her beige cargo pants were already dusted gray, and her white Nike shoes scraped against the ground as she stumbled forward. The weight of her black backpack pulled at her shoulders, making each breath feel heavier. The smoke thickened faster than she could react, swirling around her until the world blurred into shifting shadows. Her lungs burned, her legs weakened, and before she could steady herself, everything faded into darkness as she collapsed onto the warm ground.

Later, the rhythmic thump of helicopter blades cut through the haze. A rescue team spotted Patricia’s unmoving form from above and quickly descended, lifting her onto a stretcher and securing her inside the rescue helicopter. As they rose into the sky, medics worked urgently to clear her airways and stabilize her breathing. By the time they reached the hospital, she was still unconscious but alive. Hours passed before her eyes finally fluttered open beneath the bright hospital lights. Disoriented but safe, she learned she had been found just in time—saved from the smoke by the team who refused to give up searching.
