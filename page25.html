Patricia Disappointed on the Couch

I slumped into the couch and just stayed there, staring at nothing. My long black hair kept falling forward, but I didn’t bother pushing it back. My brown eyes felt tired, like they didn’t want to focus on anything anyway. The red long sleeved shirt I’d thrown on this morning suddenly felt too bright for how I was feeling, and my beige cargo pants were all bunched up under me because I’d basically dropped onto the cushions without thinking. Even my white Nikes, which I usually keep spotless, looked a little scuffed. I guess that matched my mood.

I pulled my knees closer and sank deeper into the couch, letting the disappointment settle in my chest. I’m not even sure what part of today hit me the hardest—maybe something I’d been excited about fell apart, or maybe someone said something that stuck with me in the worst way. Whatever it was, it left me feeling heavy and quiet. So I just sat there, letting the room stay still around me, hoping that if I stayed still long enough, the feeling would loosen its grip.
