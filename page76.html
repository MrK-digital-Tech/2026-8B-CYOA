Chasing a Dream

Patricia hadn’t seen her uncle in years, so when she finally found him, it felt almost unreal. He wasn’t living an ordinary life—he had followed his dream of becoming a photographer, traveling to places she had only ever seen online. His home was filled with stunning images: snowy mountains, busy city streets, quiet sunsets in places she couldn’t even name. As he showed her his work, he didn’t just talk about the photos—he told her about the risks he took, the times he almost gave up, and the moment he decided to truly commit to what he loved. Patricia listened closely, realizing this wasn’t just about photography—it was about believing in something enough to chase it.

Sitting there, she began to think about her own dreams, the ones she usually brushed off as “maybe someday.” Her uncle smiled as if he could read her thoughts and told her that dreams don’t wait for the perfect moment — you must start, even when you’re unsure. For the first time, Patricia felt something shift inside her, like a door opening to possibilities she hadn’t seriously considered before. She didn’t have everything figured out, but she understood one important thing: if she was willing to work for it and believe in herself, her future didn’t have to be small or predictable. It could be something incredible.
