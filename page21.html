Volcano is rumbling

Patricia stood at the base of the volcano, her long black hair whipping in the rising wind as the ground beneath her feet gave a low, uneasy rumble. Her brown eyes widened, reflecting the jagged black rock surrounding her and the uncertainty curling in her chest. She pulled her red long sleeved shirt closer as waves of heat pulsed from the earth, her beige cargo pants brushing against her legs with each shift of her stance. Dust clung stubbornly to her white Nike shoes, and her black backpack thumped lightly against her back as another tremor rolled through the ground. The volcano wasn’t erupting yet, but it was waking up, and Patricia could feel every warning in her bones.

She lifted her gaze toward the summit, where a thin plume of smoke spiralled into the sky like a beckoning signal. Part of her felt drawn to climb, to see what was happening at the top, to chase the answers waiting in the rising heat. But another part urged her to circle around the volcano instead, to keep a safer distance and search for another path or clue. The rumbling grew louder, vibrating through her legs, and she hesitated, caught between curiosity and caution. It felt like a crossroads—ascend into danger for clarity or move around the mountain and hope the safer route still led somewhere meaningful.
